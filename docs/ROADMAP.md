# Development plans

These are development priorities, not promised dates.

## Current

- Test 0.75's Cube Orbit EQ and Square settings menus on PS4.
- Check the updated Music browser, HUD behavior and automatic preset changes.
- Continue testing preset compatibility, visual fidelity, performance and app shutdown.
- Prepare the first public package and release notes.

## Planned polish

- Improve large-library browsing and album navigation.
- Expand saved visualizer settings.
- Improve music rescanning without reopening the app.
- Curate visually varied MilkDrop presets and make unsupported features easier to understand.
- Broaden testing across PS4 models, firmware and GoldHEN versions.

System-wide sound capture, background playback through Sony's Quick Menu, and universal MilkDrop compatibility are research ideas, not current features.
