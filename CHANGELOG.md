# Changelog

## 0.84 — changes since 0.79

Builds 0.80–0.83 were local development builds. This entry covers their changes together with 0.84.

- Added volume and mute controls to the playback HUD, beside Auto.
- Added a narrow vertical volume slider. Up/Down adjusts volume, Circle mutes/unmutes, and X closes.
- Added a five-band audio equalizer in Settings: 60 Hz, 250 Hz, 1 kHz, 4 kHz and 12 kHz, adjustable from −12 to +12 dB.
- EQ uses vertical faders. Left/Right selects a band, Up/Down adjusts it, Square resets to Flat, and Triangle toggles EQ.
- Volume, mute and EQ settings are saved. These controls affect playback audio without changing visualizer sensitivity.
- Organized Settings into Music, Visualizers, Theme and Misc tabs.
- Updated Modern HUD icons and retained pixel icons in Retro.
- Simplified Shuffle, Repeat and Auto to icons. Grey indicates Off; the theme accent indicates On. Repeat One displays a small 1.
- Fixed the FPS drop while the Modern playback HUD was visible.
- Centred selection highlights around HUD icons in both styles.
- Fixed the Auto menu showing On when automatic preset cycling was actually Off.
- Turning Auto off cancels a pending automatic preset switch without cancelling manual selections.
- Added a controller OPTIONS shortcut symbol beside the Modern settings icon.

Existing appearance settings, favourites and recent presets remain compatible.

Known issue: the previously reported occasional console shutdown when closing the app remains unresolved.

Close HYPNOTIC before installing an update.

## 0.79

- Added separate Retro and Modern interface styles.
- Expanded to nine colour palettes and six fonts, with font choice saved separately for each style.
- Modern includes rounded opaque panels, compact tabs and a circular play/pause button.
- Fixed clipped Shuffle, Repeat and Auto labels with Dot Matrix and other wider fonts.
